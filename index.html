Weather Conditions

Check current conditions before heading out. Bay Area weather can change quickly, especially near the coast.

⚠️ Fog Alert

Morning fog is common May through June. Plan rides after 10 AM for clearer conditions.

Seasonal Tips

  • Spring: Wildflowers, mild temperatures, occasional rain
  • Summer: Cool mornings, fog by afternoon, perfect for early rides
  • Fall: Warmest months, clear skies, ideal riding conditions
  • Winter: Rainy season, mud on trails, check trail closures

Safety Guidelines

🚨 Always

  • Wear a properly fitted helmet
  • Use front and rear lights
  • Follow traffic laws and signs
  • Ride with the flow of traffic
  • Carry identification and emergency contact

🎒 Recommended Gear

  • Water (minimum 1 liter)
  • Basic repair kit with spare tube
  • Phone with charged battery
  • High-visibility clothing
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
